# Mythwar — the pitch

## In one line
**Three lanes. Five mythic ages. One citadel left standing.**

## In one breath (the elevator)
Mythwar is *Age of War* for a phone in your hand and a friend on your sofa: summon creatures out
of myth down three lanes, answer every foe with the creature that beats it, and climb from fae
skirmishes to a war of gods until one citadel falls. Ten to fifteen minutes a battle, nothing to install,
and the friend can be on an iPhone while you're on a laptop.

## In thirty seconds (to someone who might play it)
Remember *Age of War* — the little Flash game where you sent cavemen and then tanks at the
other base? Mythwar is that idea grown up. There are three lanes instead of one, so you're always
reading where the next attack is coming from. Every creature has a rival it beats — the oak
treant flattens goblin packs, the goblins run down the pixies, the pixies' sparks break the
treants — so a battle is a quick game of rock-paper-scissors played across three fronts at once.
You climb through five ages of myth — the Fae Wilds, Olympus, Asgard, the Jade Empire, the
Celestial heavens — each with new creatures, a god power and a fortress to match. Puck, Hippolyta,
Odin and Ares wait at the end of each chapter. Or open it on two phones on the same Wi-Fi and fight
each other.

## Why it's worth the time (the hooks)
- **Readable depth:** one rule (*the right creature beats the wrong one*), three lanes, and
  every system on the screen — the attack you saw coming is the one you can answer.
- **Escalation:** each age changes the whole world — sky, fortress, music, creatures, god power.
- **Characters:** four generals out of myth with a voice and a history between them, a mentor
  at your side, creatures with tempers that earn names when they become veterans.
- **Together:** versus or co-op on the same Wi-Fi, PC ↔ phone, iOS ↔ Android; handicaps so a
  newcomer and a veteran can still have a close battle.
- **Made with care:** every creature, castle and effect drawn in code, every sound synthesized —
  no asset files at all.

## Store text
**Short (80 characters):** Summon creatures of myth down three lanes. Topple their citadel.

**Long:**
Out-think a rival general in a tug-of-war of legends. Summon treants, centaurs, valkyries,
kitsune and phoenixes down three lanes; counter every foe with the creature that beats it; seize
the Ley Shrines for gold; and ascend through five mythic ages to a final clash of gods.

• Four AI generals out of myth — Puck, Hippolyta, Odin and Ares — one war in four chapters
• 16 creatures with tempers, 5 god powers, 10 turrets, Ley Surges, veterans with names
• Riddles of war, Trials, Quick battles, a Sandbox and a Bestiary of the myths behind it all
• Battle a friend on the same Wi-Fi — PC, iPhone, Android, any mix — versus or together
• Colour-blind mode, full keyboard and gamepad control, no install, works offline
